About

I’m Nicolas Nino (NN), a simulation engineer focused on accurate quadrotor dynamics, robust controllers, and reproducible pipelines. I build in Unreal Engine 5 with C++ and ROS 2, and I care about clean interfaces, unit consistency, and alignment between sim and real-world behavior.

Recent work includes re-architecting a full UE5 drone sim with cascaded PID and UI tooling, training PPO agents for navigation tasks, and wiring ROS 2/ZeroMQ bridges for telemetry and control.
