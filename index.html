About

Nicolas Nino Fernandez is a Data Science student at the University of Central Florida with academic interests in machine learning, reinforcement learning, robotics, control theory, and physics-based modeling. His focus is on connecting theoretical methods with practical implementation, aiming to build systems that remain consistent from simulation to reality.

Current work centers on Unity QuadSim: a modular quadrotor research simulator with a Python SDK, deterministic experiment control, PX4 integration, visual reinforcement learning, multi-agent scenarios, and complete external-controller replacement. The same work extends into geometric control, adaptive ResNet disturbance compensation, and ROS 2 / PX4 deployment paths.
