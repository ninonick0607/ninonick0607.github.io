Unreal Project — Full Breakdown

A modular Unreal Engine 5 framework for quadrotor simulation, controls, and integrations.

Dynamics

Drone Model

Rigid‑body physics, tuned mass/inertia, thrust/drag curves, prop response, NED alignment.

Controls

Cascaded PID

Rate → angle → velocity → position loops with anti‑windup and derivative filtering.

Failure Modes

Disturbances

Hooks for wind, dropouts, and motor loss to test controller robustness.

Sensors

Noise Models

IMU/GPS/Baro/Mag with bias, drift, and configurable covariance for estimators.

Integration

PX4

HITL/SITL bridges, MAVLink mapping, and sim‑time synchronization.

Middleware

ROS 2

Publish/subscribe for telemetry and control; launch‑friendly config.

I/O

ZeroMQ

Lightweight sockets for logging, tools, and reinforcement learning loops.

UI

HUD & Tools

Minimal overlays for state, profiling, and debugging without affecting cadence.

Roadmap

Next

Weather/wind fields, customizable HUD, expanded test harness, sim‑to‑real studies.
